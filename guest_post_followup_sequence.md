# Guest-post outreach follow-up sequence

Use one message at a time and personalize every email. Stop contacting a prospect if they ask not to be contacted.

## Initial pitch
**Subject:** Guest post idea for [Publication Name] — [Topic]

Hi [Name],

I enjoyed your article on [specific article] because [genuine reason]. I’m reaching out from Brewing Ideaz, a brand design and digital agency serving businesses in Bengaluru and Kolkata.

Would you be open to a practical guest article titled “[Proposed Title]”? It would cover [one-sentence value proposition] for [their audience]. I can send a brief outline first and follow your editorial and linking guidelines.

Would this fit your upcoming content calendar?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Brewing Ideaz
[Email] | [Phone]

## Follow-up 1 — 5 to 7 days later
**Subject:** Re: Guest post idea for [Publication Name]

Hi [Name],

I wanted to follow up on my guest article idea, “[Proposed Title].” The article would give your readers [specific benefit] and would be written exclusively for [Publication Name].

If guest contributions are not currently open, please let me know. Otherwise, I would be happy to send a short outline for review.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Follow-up 2 — 7 days later
**Subject:** One alternative topic for [Publication Name]

Hi [Name],

I’m following up once more with an alternative idea that may better suit your audience: “[Alternative Title].” It would cover [brief angle] and include practical examples rather than promotional material.

If this is relevant, may I send the outline? If not, no problem—I won’t send further follow-ups.

Regards,
[Your Name]

## Accepted / next steps
**Subject:** Re: Guest post — outline and next steps

Hi [Name],

Thank you for your reply. I’ve attached/shared the outline for “[Title].” Please confirm the preferred word count, formatting, author bio requirements, and linking policy before I prepare the draft.

I’ll ensure the article is original, useful to your readers, and aligned with your editorial guidelines.

Best,
[Your Name]

## After publication
**Subject:** Thank you for publishing “[Article Title]”

Hi [Name],

Thank you for publishing the article. I’ve reviewed the live page here: [Live URL]. I appreciate the opportunity to contribute to your readers.

Please let me know if any details need correction. We’ll also share the article through our relevant channels.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Rules
- Personalize the first line and proposed topic.
- Do not send more than two follow-ups after the initial pitch.
- Do not promise rankings or demand an exact-match anchor.
- Confirm whether links are editorial, sponsored, or nofollow before publication.
