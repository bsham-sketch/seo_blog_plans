# Brewing Ideaz SEO Backlink Campaign
## Client Proposal — Phase 1

### Objective
Build relevant, trustworthy backlinks to the 10 priority SEO keyword pages already defined in the SEO plan. The campaign is designed to improve topical relevance, local visibility, referral traffic, and search visibility without using spammy or mass-produced links.

### Recommended scope
**Primary:** the 10 target keyword pages covering brand design, digital marketing, web design, web development, and website design in Bangalore and Kolkata.

**Not included in Phase 1:** a full-site backlink campaign for every homepage, service, portfolio, about, and contact URL. Those pages can be added in Phase 2 after the client approves expanded scope.

### Work included
- Confirm the final URL for each of the 10 target pages
- Optimize and verify Google Business Profile and core business citations
- Build consistent local listings for Bangalore and Kolkata
- Research relevant design, marketing, startup, technology, and local publications
- Send personalized guest-post and resource-link outreach
- Follow up professionally with interested prospects
- Record prospect, status, anchor text, live URL, and link status in the tracker
- Review link quality and remove unsuitable opportunities from consideration

### Quality and safety standards
We will prioritize relevant websites, real editorial value, natural anchor text, and consistent business information. We will not rely on link farms, automated submissions, irrelevant sites, keyword stuffing, or guaranteed-ranking claims.

### 30-day delivery plan
- **Days 1–7:** URL verification, tracking setup, Google Business Profile, and citation preparation
- **Days 8–14:** local and industry directory submissions
- **Days 15–21:** personalized outreach to qualified publications
- **Days 22–30:** follow-ups, placement coordination, live-link verification, and reporting

### Reporting
The tracker will show outreach activity, responses, accepted placements, published links, target pages, anchor text, and next actions. Results depend on publisher relevance, editorial approval, and the quality of the destination content; backlinks cannot ethically be guaranteed.
