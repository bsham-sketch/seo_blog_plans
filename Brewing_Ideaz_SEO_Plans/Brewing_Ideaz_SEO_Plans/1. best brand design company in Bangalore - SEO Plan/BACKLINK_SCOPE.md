# Backlink Scope Decision

## Chosen scope

This keyword plan should prioritize backlinks to the target SEO keyword page only.

### Final decision
The best scope for this project is: backlinks for the 10 SEO keyword pages/blogs in the repository, not the entire Brewing Ideaz website.

This means the main backlink campaign should target pages such as:
- best brand design company in Bangalore
- best brand design company in Kolkata
- best digital marketing agency in Bangalore
- best digital marketing agency in Kolkata
- best web design company in Bangalore
- best web design company in Kolkata
- best web development company in Bangalore
- best web development company in Kolkata
- best website design company in Bangalore
- best website design company in Kolkata

### Scope expansion rule
Only expand to full-site backlinks if the client explicitly asks for:
- homepage
- service pages
- portfolio page
- about page
- contact page
- general website authority building

### Recommendation
Use the 10 SEO pages as the primary backlink target. Full-site backlinks are secondary and should only be added if specifically requested.
