# 30-Day Backlink Plan for the 10 SEO Keywords

## Objective
Build targeted backlinks for the 10 keyword pages in the repository while keeping the focus on high-quality, relevant links rather than broad website-wide link building.

## Target pages
1. Best brand design company in Bangalore
2. Best brand design company in Kolkata
3. Best digital marketing agency in Bangalore
4. Best digital marketing agency in Kolkata
5. Best web design company in Bangalore
6. Best web design company in Kolkata
7. Best web development company in Bangalore
8. Best web development company in Kolkata
9. Best website design company in Bangalore
10. Best website design company in Kolkata

## Week 1: Setup and foundation
- Finalize list of the 10 key target URLs
- Set up tracking sheet and status columns
- Verify Google Business Profile details
- Update NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all relevant profiles
- Create a master list of local directories and citation sources

### Actions
- Google Business Profile optimization
- Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Clutch, Bing Places, and other local business listings
- Ensure all business details are consistent across the web

## Week 2: Directories and local citations
- Submit to local directories for Bengaluru and Kolkata
- Add business profiles to niche and industry directories
- Collect screenshots or confirmation links for each submission
- Build 5–10 relevant local citations per city

### Goal
Create a stable local authority base before outreach-heavy guest posting starts.

## Week 3: Guest post outreach
- Shortlist 3–5 realistic prospects per keyword
- Send personalized guest post pitches
- Offer topic ideas with relevance to design, marketing, branding, and web services
- Ask for a contextual link where appropriate

### Outreach focus
- design blogs
- business and startup sites
- local city blogs
- marketing and branding publications
- technology and business resource pages

## Week 4: Follow-up, link acquisition, and review
- Follow up with non-responders after 5–7 days
- Track which sites accepted the pitch and which are pending
- Record live link URLs and anchor text used
- Review high-performing keywords and target pages with the strongest link opportunities
- Adjust the next cycle based on results

## Weekly output targets
- 2–3 directory listings per keyword area
- 3–5 guest post pitches per keyword cluster
- 1–2 follow-up rounds per campaign
- Spreadsheet updated every 48 hours

## Best practice
Focus on link quality and relevance. One link from a relevant design or business publication is more valuable than ten unrelated or low-quality links.

## Final recommendation
Begin with the 10 keyword pages as the priority target. Full-site link building should be a separate stage if the client explicitly wants homepage and service-page authority building.
